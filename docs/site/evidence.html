The evidence of a migration

What migkit report exports for a hop, and every run for its own run: what was done and what was measured.

What migkit did on this hop and what it measured: what was moved, every verdict with the counts and digests of both sides and the way to take each again, the positions, the ways the engine chose and why, approvals, repairs, timings and outages, and the record they are chained in. Not a certificate: it claims only what was measured, and anyone can take each number again.

The record's keys

evidence.json holds, at its top level: format_version, what, tool, tool_version, hop, goal, scope, session, generated, route, assessment, moved, verdict, checks, tables, method, code, positions, decisions, approvals, repairs, cutovers, conflicts, timings, removed, steps, movers, record, at_rest.

The numbers of every table

tables.csv, a row for each table: db, table, where, status, method, src_engine, src_rows, src_digest, src_keys, dst_engine, dst_rows, dst_digest, dst_keys, salt, at, session.
